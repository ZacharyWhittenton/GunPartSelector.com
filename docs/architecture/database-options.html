Serverless PostgreSQL: choose for this workload, not fashion.

The site is low traffic, cost-sensitive, and initially stores contact submissions. Fast wake-up and low idle cost matter more than maximum throughput.

Recommendation: Neon for phase 1, pending client approval of a third-party data processor.

Neon’s compute suspends after inactivity and typically reactivates within hundreds of milliseconds. That fits a public contact form better than Aurora’s documented roughly 15-second typical resume. Keep Aurora as the fallback when AWS-only governance outweighs latency and cost.

Decision checklist

  1. Confirm whether client contact data may be stored with a third-party provider.
  2. Choose the data region, retention period, backup window, and deletion policy.
  3. Measure acceptable cold-query latency for the estimate form.
  4. Confirm monthly budget and whether a paid support/SLA tier is required.
  5. Set the Pulumi databaseProvider and encrypted databaseUrl.
  6. Run the first Alembic migration before enabling the production form.
