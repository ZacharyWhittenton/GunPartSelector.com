A build configurator for the AR-15 platform

GunPartSelector.com helps you plan an AR-15 build the way it should be planned: by category, with real compatibility checks, instead of a spreadsheet full of browser tabs.

Browse by category

Uppers, lowers, barrels, handguards, optics, and everything in between, filterable by brand, caliber, and spec.

02

Build with confidence

As you add parts, we flag caliber, buffer-tube, handguard-interface, and gas-system mismatches before you buy anything.

03

Share your build

Every build gets a link you can save or send — to a friend, a gunsmith, or your future self.

How we make money

GunPartSelector.com is an affiliate site: when a "Buy" link takes you to a retailer and you make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We don't hold inventory or ship parts ourselves — every purchase happens directly with the retailer.

A note on compliance

GunPartSelector.com doesn't sell firearms, and nothing on this site is legal advice. It's your responsibility to make sure any build you assemble complies with all applicable federal, state, and local law — including regulated items like short-barreled rifles and any parts subject to serialization or FFL transfer.
