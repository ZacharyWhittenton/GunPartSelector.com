Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

This policy explains what information WD Web Solutions ("we," "us") collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to visitors, registered clients, and administrators of this site.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may collect:

  • Contact form submissions: name, email address, phone number, company, and project details.
  • Account information: if you register, your name, email address, and password (stored as a one-way hash, never in plain text).
  • Scheduling: if you book a consultation, your name, email address, and any notes you provide.
  • Blog activity: comments you post and the tags you subscribe to, tied to your account.
  • Marketplace activity: items in your cart (stored only in your browser, not on our servers), wishlist entries, and order history (item, quantity, price, and order status).
  • Usage data: pages visited and general interaction data, collected automatically if analytics is enabled (see "Cookies, Local Storage & Analytics" below).

AI Features

This site may offer an AI-powered chat assistant built on Anthropic's Claude models. When you use it, your messages — and, if you're logged in, relevant account context such as your name, appointments, or order history needed to answer your question — are sent to Anthropic to generate a response. We do not use your conversations to train any AI model. Responses are generated automatically and may occasionally be incomplete or inaccurate; they are provided for informational convenience only and are not professional, legal, or financial advice. Please verify anything important with us directly before relying on it.

Payment Processing

Marketplace purchases are processed by Stripe through Stripe Checkout. Your card details are entered directly on Stripe's hosted payment page and never pass through or get stored on our servers. We receive confirmation of your order and its status, but not your full card number. Stripe's use of your information is governed by Stripe's own privacy policy.

Cookies, Local Storage & Analytics

We use your browser's local storage (not cookies) to keep you signed in and to remember items in your cart between visits. This data stays on your device and is cleared when you sign out or clear your browser storage. If Google Analytics is enabled on this site, it collects general usage data (pages visited, approximate location, device type) using its own cookies; see Google's privacy policy for details. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using Google's browser add-on. Separately, we run our own first-party usage analytics: a randomly generated identifier is stored in your browser's local storage (not a cookie, and not linked to your name, email, or account) to record which pages you visit and the general area of the page you click on. This is used only in aggregate to understand how visitors use the site and is never sold or shared with third parties.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to respond to inquiries, prepare estimates, deliver and manage the services you request (bookings, orders, blog comments), operate your account, and improve this website. We do not use your information for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers necessary to run this site — currently Stripe (payments), Anthropic (AI chat responses, if enabled), and Google Analytics (site usage, if enabled) — or when required to comply with the law.

Data Retention

We keep account, order, and appointment records for as long as your account is active and as needed to meet our legal and accounting obligations. Contact form submissions are retained only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights and Choices

You can review or update your account details at any time while logged in. To request a copy or deletion of your personal information, email us at the address below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the CCPA or GDPR; we will honor requests to the extent required by applicable law.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our services change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
